The thesis

  1. Balance sheet room. Centerra's net cash funds the 25% cash portion and absorbs Ero's notes; pro forma leverage is 1.0x 2027E EBITDA.
  2. Complementary metal mix. Centerra is gold-heavy and its copper is partly streamed; Ero adds two unstreamed Brazilian copper mines.
  3. Mine life and jurisdiction. Ero's copper replaces Öksüt's cash flow as it runs out, and dilutes Centerra's Türkiye exposure.
  4. The honest cost. At our below-spot deck the deal is −14% to NAV per share; it breaks even on NAV as copper approaches spot (US$6.48/lb). Both stocks trade at similar P/NAV (2.38x and 2.36x), so the premium is what synergies must pay for.

How it is built

  • Data pipeline: scripts/fetch_market_data.py pulls prices, shares, balance sheets, CAD/USD and COMEX copper and gold through yfinance into timestamped CSVs.
  • Model: generated from CSVs with live formulas; the copper sensitivity is produced by re-running the workbook under each price case.
  • Reconciliation: a Python engine mirrors the NAV and merger math and a test suite checks it against Excel within 0.5%.
  • Deck: every chart and figure is read from the recalculated model; nothing is typed by hand.
